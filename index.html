VoiceWeave Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 November 2026. Developer: Mosaic Digital. App: VoiceWeave (package com.nilesoftai.speaktype).

Summary

VoiceWeave is a dictation app. Your speech is converted to text on your device. VoiceWeave has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. The developer does not collect, receive, or sell your audio, text, or personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). Used only while you are dictating, to turn your speech into text. Audio is processed on your device and is not saved. Recordings are not sent to the developer.
  • Accessibility service. The optional "VoiceWeave floating mic" accessibility service lets the app find the text field you are using and insert your dictated text. It reads limited information about the focused editable field (such as the text around the cursor) only to insert and position your dictation. It skips password and other sensitive fields that Android identifies, and it stays off in a built-in list of banking and payment apps and in any apps you exclude. The service does not log, store, or transmit what it sees.
  • Input method. The optional VoiceWeave keyboard is used only for dictation in fields that do not support accessibility insertion.

Data stored on your device

VoiceWeave stores the following only in private app storage on your device: your settings, your custom dictionary entries, the app-exclusion list, downloaded speech packs, and your most recent finished transcripts (up to 25) so you can recover them from the home screen. Audio is not retained. You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Network use

  • When you choose to download an offline language pack, the app downloads it over HTTPS from public hosting (such as GitHub or Hugging Face) and verifies it with a SHA-256 checksum. Those hosts may see your IP address and standard request information, as with any download. No audio or text is uploaded.
  • For some languages, the app uses the Android on-device speech recognizer provided by your phone. Its behavior, including any language pack downloads it performs, is governed by your device's speech provider and its privacy policy.

Sharing

VoiceWeave does not share your data with third parties, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Children

VoiceWeave is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

You can revoke microphone permission, turn off the accessibility service, switch keyboards, or uninstall the app at any time in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
